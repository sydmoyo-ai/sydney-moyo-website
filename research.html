Questions that cross boundaries

My work investigates aquatic–terrestrial subsidies, wetland and coastal food webs, biodiversity conservation, and contaminants in aquatic ecosystems. I use stable isotopes, fatty acids, ecological modeling, and field studies to connect processes across ecosystem boundaries.
