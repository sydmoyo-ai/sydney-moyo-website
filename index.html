Welcome

Connecting ecosystems, scales, and ideas.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at Louisiana State University. My research brings together field ecology, stable isotopes, fatty-acid biomarkers, and ecological modeling to study environmental change across freshwater, coastal, and terrestrial ecosystems.

Research themes

Questions that cross boundaries.

Aquatic–terrestrial connections

How emerging aquatic insects and resource subsidies connect freshwater habitats to terrestrial food webs.

Food webs in a changing world

How hydroperiod, river management, and climate shape trophic interactions in wetlands and coastal marshes.

Biodiversity & contaminants

Using invertebrates and ecological tracers to understand freshwater biodiversity and contaminant exposure.
