Welcome

Connecting ecosystems, scales, and ideas.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at Louisiana State University. My research broadly focuses on understanding connections across ecosystems and the processes that structure aquatic food webs.

I combine field studies, stable isotopes, fatty-acid biomarkers, contaminant analysis, and ecological modeling to investigate freshwater, coastal, and terrestrial systems.

Research themes

Questions that cross boundaries.

Our research connects the movement of resources across ecosystems with the structure of food webs and the ecological consequences of environmental change.

Cross-Ecosystem Resource Flows

Tracing energy, nutrients, and contaminants between aquatic and terrestrial ecosystems, including the role of emerging insects and other resource subsidies.

Food Web Structure and Dynamics

Understanding trophic interactions and resource pathways in freshwater and coastal ecosystems, including restored marshes and seasonal wetlands.

Environmental Change and Ecological Responses

Investigating how land use, hydrology, climate, and other environmental pressures influence biodiversity and ecological interactions.
