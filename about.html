Ecology across boundaries

My research examines connections among freshwater, coastal, and terrestrial ecosystems. I am an Assistant Professor in Biological Sciences at Louisiana State University. I combine field studies, ecological tracers, and quantitative approaches to investigate biodiversity and environmental change.
